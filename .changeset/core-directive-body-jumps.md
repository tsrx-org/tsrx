'@tsrx/solid': patch

Report a `break` or `continue` that would jump out of an `@try`, `@pending`, `@catch` or `@empty` body, or out of a nested `@{ … }` block, as TSRX2036 or TSRX2037, and a `continue` that would leave an `@switch` case as TSRX2037. Every target runs those bodies inside functions, so the jump couldn't reach the loop, `switch` or label outside, and the compiled output didn't parse; a `break` to a loop, `switch` or label inside the body, or a `continue` to a loop inside it, still works. A `break` nested in an `@switch` case, such as `if (done) break;`, is now TSRX2008, as a `break` right in the case already was; Solid's output didn't parse, and the other targets rendered nothing for the `@switch` when the `break` ran. A code-only `@{ … }` child of a returned fragment, as in `return <>@{ if (x) continue; }<b /></>` in a loop, is now held to the same rule: every target inlined it before the `return`, so the jump reached the loop, but it is now TSRX2036 or TSRX2037, as a `var` there already was TSRX2030. A `break` or `continue` in a loop in an `@for` body that leaves the body, such as `break outer`, is now TSRX2006 or TSRX2007, and a jump that leaves an `@else if` body, or the `@else` after one, is now TSRX2003 or TSRX2004 on React, Preact, Vue and Hono too, as on Solid; both compiled to output that didn't parse. A jump in an `@if` or `@for` nested in an `@if` is now reported once, with the nested one's error. A `break` or `continue` in a function's `@{ … }` body that targets a loop or label outside the function is now TS1105 or TS1104, as in a `{ … }` body.
